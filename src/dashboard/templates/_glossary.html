What the terms on this page mean
Form line
The numbered line on IRS Form 1040-SR where an amount goes. Line 1a is wages, for example, and line 34 is the refund.
Box
The numbered box on a tax document that an amount was copied from. Box 1 of a W-2 is wages, tips and other compensation.
W-2, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-R, SSA-1099
Tax documents the taxpayer received: wages from an employer (W-2), bank interest (1099-INT), dividends (1099-DIV), pensions and IRA payouts (1099-R), and Social Security benefits (SSA-1099).
Adjusted gross income
Total income minus a few specific adjustments (form line 11a). Many other amounts depend on it.
Standard deduction
A fixed amount taken off income before tax is worked out. It is larger for people 65 or older and for people who are blind.
Taxable income
The income that tax is charged on (form line 15), after deductions.
Refund or amount owed
Tax already paid, mostly through withholding, compared with the tax due. Paying more gives a refund; paying less leaves an amount to pay.
Show the calculation
Every amount is worked out by SilverFile's calculator from the documents and the taxpayer's answers. The AI never estimates a number. Open a calculation to see each step.
Text recognition (OCR)
Reading the printed numbers from a photo or scan of a document.
Review
A second, separate check that works out every line again and compares the results before anyone sees the draft.
Policy rule
A rule from SilverFile's rule book, such as R-FS-02. Select it to read the rule in full.
