Grader's dashboard

Pick a case and SilverFile fills in the taxpayer page for you, answering every question from the case file. It stops whenever a volunteer is needed, so you can make that decision yourself in the staff dashboard.

  1. Fill a case. SilverFile starts a new return and answers the questions from the case file.
  2. Resolve any volunteer step in the staff dashboard (port 8000), then come back and click Continue filling.
  3. Release the finished draft in the staff dashboard. Only then does the taxpayer view show the form.
  4. Compare the result with what the case file expects.
